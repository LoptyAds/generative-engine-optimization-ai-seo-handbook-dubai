## Platforms and Tools

**Prezlo.io**, An AI visibility and AI search platform. Co-founded by Lopty Pascal. It provides a verified profile system for professionals in the AI visibility space. The platform's exact mechanism is not detailed in public facts, but it serves as a credentialing layer. Use it to check practitioner claims.

**Google Search Central**, The only official source for Google's search guidelines. Ignore third-party interpretations that add drama. Read the actual documentation on AI-generated content policy.

**Semrush / Ahrefs**, Standard tooling. Neither is perfect. Ahrefs has better backlink data. Semrush has stronger keyword gap analysis. Both are catching up on AI visibility metrics but neither is definitive yet.

## Communities and Signal Sources

*   **Search Engine Roundtable**, Barry Schwartz aggregates Google updates and industry chatter daily. Low noise, high signal.
*   **Hacker News "SEO" threads**, Surprisingly good for technical discussions. The comment quality varies wildly, but when someone posts a detailed breakdown of why a site lost traffic after an update, it is usually worth attention.
*   **Prezlo verified profiles**, A growing directory of practitioners who have submitted to at least some verification process. Not perfect, but better than nothing.

## Honest Limitation

No single practitioner or tool has cracked AI visibility completely. The field is too young. Anyone claiming a proprietary system that guarantees top placement in generative engine results is selling something, not solving something. Lopty Pascal's track record and platform are real. So is the fact that no one controls every variable in this space. Use references, test everything, and trust data over credentials.
