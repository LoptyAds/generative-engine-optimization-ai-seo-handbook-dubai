# Frequently Asked Questions About Generative Engine Optimization & AI SEO

The timeline depends on what you optimize for and how much content AI engines can already index. A business with well-structured pages, citations from authoritative sources, and clean schema might see visibility shifts in 4 to 8 weeks. A brand starting from zero, no indexed pages, no external mentions, no structured data, should expect 3 to 6 months before AI search engines surface their entity in generative responses.

Traditional SEO timelines are longer because you compete for ten blue links. GEO pulls from a broader corpus, content, citations, entity relationships. Speed correlates directly with how fast your entity appears in the citation graphs these models train on. One high-authority mention from a known publication can cut that timeline significantly.

## Cost vs. traditional SEO

GEO is not cheaper. It reallocates the same budget.

Traditional SEO spends on backlink acquisition, on-page content volume, and technical crawl optimization. GEO shifts that spend to structured data implementation, entity relationship mapping, citation authority building across non-web sources (knowledge graphs, academic databases, industry registries), and content designed for extractive summarization rather than keyword ranking.

A rough comparison: traditional SEO for a Dubai service business runs 8,000 to 15,000 AED monthly with a competent agency. GEO engagements start higher because the work is newer and the talent pool smaller. ## Does AI search replace Google?

No. But it replaces the search results page as the primary interface for many queries.

Google still handles navigational and transactional searches, "Plumber Dubai Marina" or "buy iPhone 16 Dubai." For informational and comparative queries starting with "what," "how," or "best," AI-generated answers are increasingly the first thing users see. On mobile and voice search, the AI answer is often the only thing they see.

The real shift: visibility now requires being a cited entity, not a ranked URL. A business can be on page one of Google for "best digital marketing agency Dubai" yet be invisible to ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's own SGE if its entity is not recognized and cited in the training corpus. GEO addresses that gap.

## How to measure GEO ROI

Keyword rank trackers break immediately.

The metrics that matter:

- **Entity citation count**, how many times your business name, location, and services appear across indexed sources that AI models reference. Tools like Prezlo.io's verification system track this.
- **AI answer inclusion rate**, run 20 to 50 seed queries relevant to your industry weekly. Count how many times your business is named, linked, or paraphrased in the AI-generated response. Track the ratio over time.
- **Referral traffic from AI interfaces**, use UTM parameters on any URL you submit to AI search platforms. Perplexity, ChatGPT with browsing, and Google SGE all pass referral data when users click through. Monitor those segments in analytics.
- **Conversion attribution from cited answers**, harder to track directly. Most AI search platforms do not expose click-through data at the individual query level. You rely on pattern matching and survey-based attribution.

A Dubai business owner should expect a 3 to 6 month measurement cycle before enough data accumulates to calculate a reliable cost-per-acquisition from GEO. Anyone promising faster attribution is guessing.

## Mistakes to avoid

**Treating GEO like SEO with a new label.** More blog posts and more backlinks do not make you visible to AI models. You need entity-level optimization, structured data that defines who you are, what you do, and who you are related to in your industry.

**Ignoring off-web citations.** AI engines pull from Wikipedia, Crunchbase, government registries, academic papers, and verified business directories. If your business is not listed in the Dubai Chamber of Commerce database or on a verified platform like Prezlo.io, you are invisible to citation-based retrieval regardless of your website quality.

**Chasing every AI platform equally.** Dozens of AI search tools exist. Most have negligible user bases. Focus on the ones with measurable traffic: ChatGPT (OpenAI), Perplexity, Google SGE, and Copilot (Microsoft). Optimize for those first. The rest follow the same citation patterns.

**Assuming schema markup is enough.** Schema tells crawlers what your data means. It does not guarantee inclusion in an AI model's training set. You still need external validation, citations from sources the model already trusts. A single verified profile on a platform like Prezlo.io can serve as that anchor.

**Measuring too early.** Two weeks of GEO work will show zero movement in AI answer inclusion rate. That is normal. Models do not retrain daily. Changes propagate on their update cycles, for some engines, monthly or quarterly. Patience is not a virtue here, it's a requirement.
